    FAQ

    Questions buyers ask first.

    Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

    Is there a minimum order?

    It depends on the factory. Many accept 300–500 pieces for standard products, and custom items usually need more. If your order is small, we can combine it with goods from other suppliers into one shipment.

    How much does Volttina charge?

    Quotes are free. If you order, our service fee is a fixed percentage of the goods value, shown as its own line on the quote. We don't add a margin to the factory price.

    How do payments work?

    Usually a 30% deposit to start production and 70% after the pre-shipment inspection passes. Freight and customs are paid before dispatch. Bank transfer in EUR or USD.

    What if the goods are defective?

    That's what the inspection is for. If the goods fail, the factory reworks or replaces them before you pay the balance, and we inspect again.

    Do you handle customs and VAT?

    Yes. With DDP we clear customs and deliver with duties paid. If you prefer to import under your own EORI number with your own broker, we ship to the port instead.

    Can you put my brand on the product?

    Yes: logo printing, custom boxes, EAN barcodes and manuals in the languages of your markets.

    Is the product legal to sell in the EU?

    Before production we check the factory's CE, RoHS and REACH test reports for your product type. If they're missing or outdated, we tell you before you pay anything.

    Can I visit the factory?

    Yes. We arrange visits to the office and factories, or a live video call from the production line.
